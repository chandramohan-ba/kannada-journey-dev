How we measure learning

We do not measure learning by the number of phrases memorised. We measure it by whether you can create your own sentences, understand responses, and participate in everyday conversations.

A typical short course gives you exposure to Kannada. Kannada Journey is designed to give you usable spoken independence. That takes more than a list of phrases: building sentences step by step, a clear understanding of how Kannada works, speaking practice, correction, revision, and real-life use. That is the depth and support the outcome needs.
