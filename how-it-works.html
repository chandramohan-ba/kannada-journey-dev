How it works

A clear path, not a heavy syllabus.

Kannada Journey is designed as a sequence of small, practical steps. You start with useful everyday language and gradually build towards clearer expression and deeper understanding.

  1. Speak
  2. Patterns
  3. Vocabulary
  4. Read
  5. Write
  6. Deepen
  7. Belong

How the journey unfolds

The pathway is organised into nine programs, grouped into three stages. Each stage builds on the previous one.

Stage 1 of 3

Speak Spoken Kannada Foundations · Programs 1–4

From your first sentences to independent everyday Kannada.
You speak from the first weeks, without learning the script first. You build sentences, add time, and then add words. You can stop here if spoken Kannada is all you need.

You will be able to

  • Say what you do, ask questions, say what you will not do, and politely ask others to do things.
  • Talk about yourself and about anyone else, in all the everyday situations.
  • Talk about yesterday, today and tomorrow, and about what is finished or going on now.
  • Use a wide everyday vocabulary and hold real conversations.
Rahul

“No letters yet?”

Anand

“Not yet. First we use your voice. The letters will make sense when you already know the words.”

Stage 2 of 3

Read & Write Written Kannada · Programs 5–8

Perfect your conversation, and enrich it through continuous practice.
With written Kannada you can read books, watch movies, enact scripts and role plays, and keep practising. The rules and the vocabulary become a part of you, and the more you read and watch, the more your vocabulary grows.

You will be able to

  • Read and write the Kannada script.
  • Read messages, signs, articles and simple books.
  • Write simple messages and paragraphs, and express any time in writing.
  • Handle longer, richer sentences, in everyday speech and in formal writing.
Rahul

“Now that I can speak, what does reading give me?”

Anand

“Books, films and messages become your teachers. Every new page brings new words.”

Stage 3 of 3

Belong Be a part of the Alumni · Program 9

Keep growing, and keep company.
You grow your vocabulary through immersion and expression, and you join an alumni community that stays with you, learning and enjoying Kannada together with other batches.

You will be able to

  • Learn new words from newspapers, films, news and stories.
  • Take part in role plays, acting and discussions.
  • Practise with newer learners and stay connected to the teacher and the community.
Rahul

“Is this the end of the journey?”

Anand

“It is the beginning of belonging. You do not finish Kannada Journey and lose touch: you join a living practice community.”
