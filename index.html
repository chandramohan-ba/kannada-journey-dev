Kannada that becomes part of life

Kannada Journey is for anyone who wants to move beyond isolated words and start using Kannada in real situations.

Practical from the start

Learn useful sentences and expressions you can use in daily life.

Step by step

Progress through clear programs without being overwhelmed by grammar.

Made for connection

Build the confidence to speak with people, not merely study a language.

Guru does not begin with rules or long lists of words. He begins where every learner stands — with the confidence to speak, a clear path to follow, and people to share the journey with.

Guru

“You'll speak from day one, learn step by step, and graduate into a community that keeps the language alive — movies, role-plays, and friends beyond the classroom.”

Rohan

“Words I can use today, a path I can trust, and friends to practice with. Let's begin.”

Your journey in seven steps

  1. Speak
  2. Patterns
  3. Vocabulary
  4. Letters
  5. Grammar
  6. Deepen
  7. Belong
  • Speak (Program 1): start speaking basic sentences
  • Patterns (Programs 2–3): learn how spoken kannada sentences are formed using simple patterns.
  • Vocabulary (Program 4): learn more everyday words
  • Letters (Program 5): learn the script
  • Grammar (Programs 6–7): understand sentence structure
  • Deepen (Program 8): master advanced grammar
  • Belong (Program 9): practice with the community

Guru shows Rohan the path ahead — seven steps, each one building on the last.

Guru

“You don't need to see the whole journey today, Rohan. Just the next step.”

Rohan

“Seven steps — and every one of them points forward.”
